# 📱 Scavenger Hunt App — Player Guide

Welcome to the hunt! This app is how you **check off challenges and upload your
proof**. Keep it open on your phone all afternoon. Here's everything you need.

---

## Getting started

**1. Open the app**
Go to **`sip-scavenger-hunt.netlify.app`** in your phone's browser (Safari or
Chrome). Tip: after it loads, use your browser's **"Add to Home Screen"** so it's
one tap away.

**2. Join your team**
- Type your **team code** (it's in your printed packet).
- Type your name (required — it shows on your team's roster).
- Tap **Join team**. Your phone remembers it — you only do this once.

> **The golden rule:** Use your **printed packet** to find and solve challenges.
> Use the **app** to check them off and upload your photo/video proof. The
> numbers in the app match the numbers in your packet.

---

## Submitting a challenge

1. Tap the challenge in the app (they're grouped into the 5 quests, and numbered
   to match your packet).
2. Tap **"Take or choose a photo/video."** You can snap a new one **or** pick one
   from your camera roll.
3. **At least 2 teammates must be in every photo or video** — this gets checked.
4. Some challenges ask for an extra detail (an exhibit name, a fact, a recorded
   answer). If so, there's a box to type it in — don't skip it.
5. Tap **Submit evidence.** Done!

**You'll see the challenge change status:**
- **Uploading… / Pending** → it's sent; the admin hasn't reviewed it yet.
- **Verified ✓** → approved! Points are on the board.
- **Rejected ✕** → not approved (you'll see a note why). You can try again.

---

## 📶 Weak signal? Don't panic.

This is built for spotty city service. When you hit Submit, your photo is
**saved on your phone and keeps trying to upload on its own** — even if you lose
signal for a while. **Nothing is lost.** It'll finish uploading when your signal
comes back or when you're back on wifi at home base. You can keep playing while
it works in the background.

---

## Reading your score

At the top of the screen:
- **Big number** = your team's official points (from **verified** challenges).
- **"X if all verified"** = where you'd be if everything pending gets approved.
- **★ Hero's Journey meter** = how many of the 5 quests you've scored in. Touch
  **3 quests → +5**, **4 → +10**, **all 5 → +15** bonus points. So it pays to
  spread out, not just grind one quest!

---

## 🎥 Video tips (important!)

Video challenges are worth the most, but big files are slow on cell service:
- **Keep clips short** — 15 to 30 seconds, like the challenge says.
- If you can, record at **1080p, not 4K** (Settings → Camera → Record Video).
- A too-big video will tell you it's over the limit — just record a shorter one.

Boston, public service, or your summer here means to you. **Tag @CityofBoston**,

## Quick fixes

- **"Couldn't reach the server / Reload"** → tap Reload, or check your signal.
- **My photo won't upload / stuck on Uploading** → that's fine, it keeps
  retrying. Big videos take a while. Move on and it'll finish.
- **I joined the wrong team** → scroll to the bottom and tap **"Joined the wrong
  team?"** to switch (you'll need the correct team's code).
- **My points aren't showing** → they only count once the admin **verifies** your
  submission. Pending doesn't count yet — that's normal.
- **The app looks stuck/weird** → fully close the browser tab and reopen the URL.

## 🏙 A few ground rules

You're representing the City of Boston today:

- Use good judgment and stay safe — no challenge is worth a risk.
- Be brief and kind to anyone you talk to; "no thank you" is a complete answer.
- Only post things that make you and the program look great.
