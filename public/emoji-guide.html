01 How Is This Even Legal?

The technical scandal underneath your relationship.

🔤Emoji are letters, not pictures

When you send 😭 you're not sending an image — you're sending the Unicode character U+1F62D, basically a fancy letter of the alphabet. His phone receives the letter and draws it with its own art. You've never once seen the same picture.

🎨Every company draws its own set

Apple has one set, Google (Noto) another, Samsung a third, and WhatsApp, Facebook, and (formerly) Twitter all drew their own too. Same characters, wildly different artistic opinions — like fonts, but emotionally load-bearing.

🌀The Samsung plot twist

If he has a Galaxy, he doesn't even see Google's versions — Samsung draws its own on top of Android. There isn't one "Android emoji." There's a whole extended universe, and your texts are being localized without your consent.

▯The tofu problem

Brand-new emoji roll out at different speeds. If his phone is behind on updates, your cutting-edge emoji arrives as a blank box (nicknamed "tofu"). That □ he ignored? That was a feeling. It died in transit.

03 The Hall of Infamy

A brief history of emoji divergence — real events, lightly dramatized.

  • April 2016
    University of Minnesota researchers publish "Blissfully Happy or Ready to Fight", scientifically confirming that the same emoji reads completely differently across platforms — with 😁 crowned the most misread character in the language. Your relationship's fate was peer-reviewed before it began.
  • August 2016
    Apple unilaterally turns the revolver 🔫 into a toy water pistol. For the next two years, an iPhone user's squirt gun arrives on other phones as an actual firearm. Possibly the highest-stakes translation error in the entire system.
  • November 2016
    Peach-gate. An iOS beta redesigns 🍑 to look like actual fruit. The internet revolts within hours. Apple restores the butt before the update even ships. Democracy works.
  • August 2017
    Android 8.0 executes the beloved "blob" emojis and replaces them with standard round faces. Millions mourn. This is also the moment Android and iPhone emoji started converging — everything before this line was the wild west.
  • October 2017
    Burger-gate. Someone notices Google's 🍔 puts the cheese underneath the patty. Google's CEO publicly promises to "drop everything" and address it. The cheese is relocated. Meanwhile, Google's 🍺 is caught with its foam floating mid-air above a half-empty glass, in defiance of physics. Also fixed. Rough season for Google's kitchen.
  • October 2018
    Bagel-gate. Apple ships a 🥯 so joyless — machine-sliced, visibly dry, no schmear — that the nation protests. Apple adds cream cheese within weeks. The same season, Google and Samsung finally holster their revolvers and go water-pistol. Peace, at last, on one front.
  • 2019 → today
    The Great Convergence. The big vendors now design defensively and the sets mostly align. But "mostly" is doing a lot of work in that sentence — the nuance gap survives, which is why this Bureau exists.
